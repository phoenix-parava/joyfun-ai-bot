Joy Platform Overview Production Grade

Joy Connect is a secure peer-to-peer audio and text communication ecosystem integrated with intelligent AI companionship. Designed from the ground up for strict privacy, memory efficiency, and low-latency interaction.

🛡️
Zero Leakage Privacy: Real Telegram user IDs, phone numbers, and profile photos are never exposed to connected peers. Server-authoritative regex sanitization blocks all phone numbers, email addresses, and external links.
🎙️
Browser-Native WebRTC: Audio flows directly peer-to-peer using high-efficiency Opus codecs or encrypted STUN/TURN relays, keeping backend server memory strictly under 500 MB.
🌐
Three-Tier Language Matrix: Seamlessly matches users across Code-Switched Bilingual Hybrids (Tanglish, Hinglish, Telugish, etc.), Pure Regional Indian Languages, and Global Languages.
💡
Bilingual Topic Icebreakers: Integrated topic deck cycling helps peers start meaningful, engaging conversations with one tap.
⚡
Instant AI Warm-Up: No idle waiting in queue — practice speaking with an intelligent companion until a human partner arrives.

📜 User Agreement & Privacy Policy

Welcome to Joy Connect. Please read this mandatory agreement carefully before continuing:

  1. Strict Prohibition of Personal Information: Users are strictly prohibited from sharing personal phone numbers, Telegram IDs, email addresses, social media links, home addresses, or personal media with any peer.
  2. Zero Liability Policy: Joy Connect and its creators assume zero responsibility or legal liability for any personal information, contacts, or external links voluntarily shared by users during voice calls or text chat.
  3. Automated Privacy Guardrails: The platform enforces strict automated regex filtering on text messages and custom pseudonyms. Any attempt to share contact details will be blocked automatically.
  4. Safe Community Standards: Harassment, hate speech, abusive language, or predatory conduct results in permanent revocation of platform access.
